The integration process is designed to be simple and efficient. Here is how it works step by step:

1. Product Sync

All product details from Square are automatically imported to Amazon. This includes product name, description, price, images, and SKU details.

2. Inventory Synchronization

3. Order Management

Orders placed on Amazon are directly reflected in Square POS. Businesses can manage fulfillment from a single system without switching platforms.

4. Real-Time Updates

Any changes made in Square such as price updates or product modifications are automatically reflected on Amazon in real time.
